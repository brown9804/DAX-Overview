# Code of conduct

Make this learning space welcoming, respectful, and useful.

- Be patient with beginners and explain technical disagreements constructively.
- Respect different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose private information, or spam.
- Share only content you are authorized to publish.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and related
project interactions. Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate contributions,
close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community.

For a conduct concern, contact organization maintainers through an available
private GitHub channel. Do not open a public issue containing private details.
If no private channel is available, report abuse through GitHub's supported
reporting tools rather than exposing the affected person.
